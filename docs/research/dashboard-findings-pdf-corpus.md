## Shortlist (13) - each with the specific method / chart / dataset, not the topic

### A. Anchors - the four confirmed leads (represented, not re-litigated)

**1. Nils's thesis (SPVR01) Ch. 4 - the primary dataset.** Chart forms already in the thesis, portable 1:1 to panels: grouped **bar charts** for accuracy & rating by modality / condition / participant / ideophone (Figs 7–14); and - the key one - **Fig 15 & 16 = accuracy × mean-rating scatterplots** (by condition, by modality). That scatter *is* the guess-vs-rating divergence panel, in his own data. Drives directly off `data/thesis-per-pair-stats.csv` (30 rows: `pairing, modality, n_correct_of36, accuracy_pct, mean_rating, median_rt_ms`). Headline numbers for KPI cards: 64.2% mean accuracy vs 50% chance; modality accuracy 6.86/6.42/5.97 (aud>vis>int); rating reversal 4.45/4.24/4.08 (int>vis>aud); per-pair spread 94%→36%. RT is a live-data-ready difficulty covariate (median 13.1s; accuracy~RT rho=−0.33).

**2. McLean & Dingemanse (2025), "A multi-methods toolkit for documentary research on ideophones"** (OUP *Capturing Expressivity* ch. 5; PDF at pure.mpg.de item_3644631). The methodology bible for the **semantic-network panel**. Concretely: similarity-judgement data (pile-sort, or triadic odd-one-out) → **MDS plot** clustering ideophones by sensory domain (Fig 5.4) → **force-directed network** with a **domain-wheel top level** (Fig 5.10: SHAPE/TEXTURE/TASTE/SOUND/APPEARANCE/STRENGTH) that expands into per-domain sub-networks (Fig 5.11). Also: tag-structured browsing (Fig 5.9, "curve"), and **sensory modality ratings** (Likert, multisensory incl. interoception) as the panel's underlying measure. Table 5.1 = strategies for representing ideophone meaning.

**3. McLean - `JapaneseMimeticNetwork`** (github.com/BonnieMcLean/JapaneseMimeticNetwork, **MIT**; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.14598876; live interactive demo linked from the record). Built with **R + `shiny` + `visNetwork`**. UX precedent to copy: force-directed network where **hover over an ideophone node → preview/link to its lexical entry**, with **images and short videos embedded in nodes**. Data model is trivially adaptable: **one nodes file + one edges file** (the whole app is a 3.2 kB zip). This is the closest existing thing to the dashboard's network panel.

**4. McLean - Siwu Ideophones network** (github.com/BonnieMcLean/SiwuIdeophoneNetwork, **MIT**; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.14598830; same shiny/visNetwork stack). The **alternative-view** precedent: opens on the **domain wheel**, **click a domain node → expand its ideophone sub-network**, **double-click a node → expand/contract clusters** while exploring. Semantic structure from Dingemanse & Majid (2012) pile-sort (senses cluster; taste is an outgroup; shape groups with texture) - a principled node hierarchy the JP panel can mirror. The Zenodo record explicitly invites reuse ("Feel free to use the code to make a similar network yourself… we simply ask that you cite the following paper").

**5. Dingemanse & Thompson - `playful_iconicity`** (confirmed lead; repo + notebooks). Chart-pattern reference: **scatterplot + density-ridgeline combos and faceted panels**. Dataset: 70,462 English words × funniness + iconicity (experimental + imputed). Low direct data use (English, off-stimulus), high value as **chart-idiom source** (ridgelines for rating distributions by group) and landing-page flavor ("funny words are iconic").

### B. Corpus additions - the triage payload

**6. McLean, Dunn & Dingemanse (2023), "Two measures are better than one"** (`papers/`, OSF data `mclean2023-measures/`). The scale-level backbone of the divergence panel. Charts: **guessing-accuracy × mean-rating scatter, colored by lexical stratum** (ideophone vs prosaic - Figs 7/8); **linear regression predicting rating from guess with a stratum×guess interaction** (Table 1 + Fig 9, and the reverse in Table 2 + Fig 10); **measure-discriminability plot** (Fig 6); **rating-consistency/reliability** (Fig 11); z-scored measures. Dataset: `GuessingRatingScores.csv`, 304 items (101 ideophones + 203 controls). **Copy correction it forces:** retire the thesis-internal "nearly orthogonal" line (rho=+.16, n=30) - at scale guess~rating is rho=+.44 overall / **+.65 within ideophones**; the real dissociation is that **ratings separate ideophones from prosaic words (Cliff's δ=+.50) while guessing does not (δ=−.01)**. Divergence panel should say "the two measures see different things," not "they're unrelated."

**7. Punselie, McLean & Dingemanse (2024), "The Anatomy of Iconicity"** (`zotero-import/`; repo `triangulating_iconicity/`, **CC BY 4.0**). A **three-measure triangulation** the dashboard can stage as one panel: **cumulative structural-analogy score (objective)** × **iconicity rating (subjective)** × **guessability (behavioral)**. Charts: **guessability × cumulative-iconicity scatter, one dot per ideophone** (Fig 3); **dot plots faceted by semantic domain** (Figs 4/6); **rating-distribution density** (Fig 5); mixed-effects models, Pearson, bootstrap CIs. Dataset: 239 ideophones × 5 languages (**81 Japanese**), per-item guessability + structural form/meaning/correspondence coding + **241 .wav included**. Table 1 = the 5-correspondence coding scheme (a principled "why is this word iconic" scaffold).

**8. Winter et al. (2024), "Iconicity ratings for 14,000+ English words"** (`papers/`; repo `iconicity_ratings/`). Dataset: 14,776 words × cleaned rating + item SD. Charts: **kernel-density plot of the rating distribution with a same-mean normal overlay** (Fig 2); **Bayesian regression coefficient plot with 95% credible intervals** (Fig 3); ranked **top/bottom-10 table** (Table 2); means by part-of-speech (Table 3); split-half reliability. Dashboard uses: the density + coefficient-plot idioms, and a **Rating-Lab calibration round** (rate a few English anchors, see yourself vs 1,400+ raters before the Japanese trials).

**9. Iida & Akita (2023), "Perceptual Strength Norms for 510 Japanese Words"** (`papers/`; data `Perceptual_strength_norms_JpnRaw.csv`, 510×17 - already the project's modality-classification source). Dataset: per-word **6-modality perceptual-strength vector** (Auditory/Visual/Haptic/Gustatory/Olfactory/Interoceptive) + dominant modality + iconicity + modality exclusivity. Charts: **modality-grouped boxplots** (freq / iconicity by dominant modality - Figs 1/3); **perceptual-strength × iconicity scatter** (Fig 2); **exclusivity by lexical category** (Fig 5); JP-vs-EN comparison (Fig 6). The 6-vector → a **radar / modality-profile chart per word** (a genuinely new axis the thesis didn't visualize). Already joined to NINJAL top-600 + script frequency on romaji key (`f1-dataset-profile.md`, Pool A=38 / B≈49).

**10. Thompson et al. (2020), "Iconicity ratings across the Japanese lexicon"** (`zotero-import/`). Chart: **jittered violin plots of iconicity rating by word-class / lexical stratum**, Japanese and English side by side (Figs 1/2); mixed-effects logistic regression. Gives the dashboard a **violin-by-category idiom** and a ready **Japanese-vs-English iconicity comparison** framing (ideophonic stratum rates highest).

**11. Motamedi et al. (2019), "The iconicity toolbox"** (`zotero-import/`). The measurement-method menu - use it to pick the right stat per panel. Interfaces: comprehension/guessing as an **n-choice array** (Fig 1), rating-task layouts (Fig 2). Viz: **bar plot of rating distribution from anti-iconic (neg) to iconic (pos)** (Fig 3); **bootstrapped confidence intervals for regression coefficients** (Fig 4). Also codifies split-half reliability and Cohen's d for iconicity measures.

**12. McLean (2021), "Revising an implicational hierarchy … Japonic"** (`papers/`). The peer-reviewed backing for the network/hierarchy panels (and the source already framing Perception Ladder: SOUND < MOVEMENT < FORM < TEXTURE < OTHER). Charts: **semantic-extension network diagram** (Fig 8); **3-D semantic map** (Fig 11); **cross-language semantic-extension comparison** (Fig 12); responses normalized by count (Fig 7). Method precedent for a "how meanings connect" panel and for using the hierarchy as an ordering axis.

**13. In-project joined datasets (not a paper, but the expansion data layer).** `f1-dataset-profile.md` already built the romaji-keyed join of NINJAL/BCCWJ top-600 (frequency + script share) × Iida-Akita 6-modality norms × SketchEngine kata/hira script frequency, plus the `mclean2023` external guess/rating anchors for 9 thesis words. This is the substrate for any dashboard panel that reaches **beyond the 60 thesis words** (frequency vs script-preference scatter; the "Convention Frontier" of high-frequency/low-transparency words). Feeds NIL-53/54.

**Method-reference shelf (keep, don't build a panel from):** `analyzing_linguistic_data.pdf` (Baayen - R mixed models, PCA), `statistics_for_linguists.pdf`, Keppel & Wickens (2004). Use for how to compute, not what to show.
